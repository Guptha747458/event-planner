### Copy & Paste Content:

Planning events often means wrestling with disorganized spreadsheets, broken formulas, and endless vendor tabs. 

Today, I’m excited to unveil **AuraPlan AI** — an intelligent event planning assistant powered by **Google Gemini** and built in the **Google Antigravity** environment! 🚀✨

Whether you’re organizing an intimate rustic wedding, a milestone 30th birthday, or a vibrant community street festival, AuraPlan transforms your vision into a structured, production-ready cockpit in seconds:

📊 **Real-Time Budget Tracker**: Interactive SVG donut chart and category sliders that recalculate dynamically. Powered by an AI refiner where you can simply say *"Cut catering by 20% and move to decor"* and watch the numbers auto-balance.
⏱️ **Milestone Timeline Generator**: Vertical phased roadmap from "Today" to "Event Day" with priority badges, status toggles, and customizable tasks.
🤝 **Vendor Checklist**: Categorized partner directory with status tracking (Not Contacted ➔ Reached Out ➔ Booked ➔ Deposit Paid) and actual vs estimated cost tallies.
💡 **Personalized Idea Generator ("Surprise Me")**: 6 creative concepts tailored to your aesthetic, paired with actionable DIY budget tips to save money without sacrificing elegance.
🖨️ **Print & Share**: Instant executive PDF/print summary and shareable cloud plan IDs for family and co-planners.

🛠️ **Tech Stack**:
• Backend: Node.js, Express, and the official `@google/genai` SDK with structured function calling schemas
• Frontend: React 19, Vite, Lucide icons, and a custom Obsidian Glassmorphic CSS design system
• Testing: Native Node.js test runner with unit and integration tests
